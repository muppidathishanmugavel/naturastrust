About Natura's Trust

Natura's Trust works for a healthier, greener and more responsible society. We believe a strong community begins with healthy children, aware young people and a clean, protected environment. Through sports, awareness and care for nature, we encourage our children and youth to choose a healthy life and to take responsibility for the place they live in.

Why We Must Stand Against Drug Abuse

Drug abuse quietly destroys health, families and futures. Prevention is always better than cure – and every one of us, at home, in school and in our neighbourhood, can help protect our young people.

  • It harms the body and the mind, and can lead to dependence.
  • It breaks families and steals education, work and dreams.
  • Young people are most at risk – curiosity and peer pressure are strong.
  • Sports, healthy routines and caring families protect our youth.
  • Dependence can be treated – asking for help early saves lives.

Need help? You are not alone

If you or someone you know needs support for drug abuse, or wants to report drug trafficking, call these free national helplines (24×7).

📞 1933 – National Narcotics Helpline (MANAS)
📞 14416 – Tele-MANAS (mental health support)

Save the Thamirabarani

The Thamirabarani – the “Porunai” of ancient Tamil literature – flows from the Western Ghats through Tirunelveli district. It is the lifeline of our region, bringing water to our fields, villages and homes.

Plastic waste, sewage and careless dumping are slowly harming our river. Protecting the Thamirabarani means protecting our drinking water, our farmers, our wildlife and the generations that come after us. What can we do?

  • Never dump plastic, garbage or waste in the river.
  • Keep sewage and chemicals out of our water.
  • Plant and protect trees along the banks.
  • Teach children to love and respect rivers.
  • Join clean-up drives and keep our surroundings clean.

Our marathon finishes on the bank of the Thamirabarani at Mukkudal – a reminder that every step we run is a step towards protecting nature.
